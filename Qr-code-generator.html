About QR Codes

What is a QR Code?
QR (Quick Response) codes are two-dimensional barcodes that store data like URLs, text, contact info, or Wi-Fi credentials. They can be scanned by any smartphone camera.
Common Uses
Website links, product packaging, digital payments, event tickets, Wi-Fi login, business cards, menu ordering, and authentication.
Error Correction
QR codes include error correction (L, M, Q, H). Higher levels allow up to 30% of the code to be damaged while still being readable — useful for logos or artistic overlays.
Privacy & Security
All processing is done in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server. QR codes are not encrypted — anyone can read what's encoded.
