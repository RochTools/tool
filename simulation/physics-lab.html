How to use

Pick a Mode
Choose Free Fall, Projectile, Collision, Pendulum, Spring or Orbit from the modes panel.
Adjust Physics
Fine-tune gravity, air resistance, elasticity and time scale. Changes apply instantly.
Tap Canvas
Tap anywhere on the canvas to spawn a new object. Great for building your own experiments.
Keyboard
Space = play/pause · R = reset · C = clear · 1–6 = switch mode
