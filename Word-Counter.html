About Word Counter

What it does
Counts words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, and lines. Also shows reading/speaking time and most used keywords.
Why use it
Perfect for writers, students, bloggers, and professionals who need to meet word limits, analyze content, or improve readability.
Reading time
Based on average reading speed of 200-250 words per minute. Speaking time is based on 130-150 words per minute.
Privacy
Everything happens in your browser. No data is ever sent to any server — your text stays completely private.
